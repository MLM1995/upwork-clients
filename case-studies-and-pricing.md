## 2. Which case to use for each type of job

- Shopify, ecommerce, food, Klaviyo, Merchant Center: MagsFood.
- Health, therapy, psychiatry, clinics: Connect Integrative Psychiatry (plus Whole Person Psychiatry and Vancouver City Hypnotherapy); for launches in sensitive sectors, Step One.
- Local SEO and Google Business Profile: Second Story Salon (15 to 3), Mission Bowling Club (first place), Pizza Miltonio.
- Implementing an audit that is already done: Miguel Baumann, Mission Bowling Club, USED CONEX.
- Traffic drops, indexing, Cloudflare: Joiner Edinburgh (524 on sitemaps) and cadblocksfree.com.
- Agencies that subcontract (white label): Makoa (Mission Bowling Club) and the Edinburgh agency (Loft Conversions, +267% clicks).
- Squarespace: Mission Bowling Club, Connect Integrative Psychiatry, Jenn Perman.
- Wix: Second Story Salon.
- WordPress, website builds: Madison View Apartments.
- Cold email and email infrastructure: DMCenter (more than 40% open rate).
- AI-built or Lovable websites: BH Property Services.

Notes for quoting:
- These amounts are the real history, not a ceiling. In 2024 and 2025 there were many jobs of 5 to 100 USD; in 2026 complete projects ranged from 375 to 1,000 USD, and the current hourly rate is 55 USD/h.
- To estimate, calculate the hours and multiply them by 55 USD; use the table as a minimum reference for similar services.
- Split the work into milestones with a written scope (like MagsFood: 600 + 400 USD). Several clients tried to add out-of-scope extras (MagsFood, Madison View, Get Blue), so define the scope in writing in the proposal.
- When the job is large, offer a closed first phase and leave the following ones as an option (like Pizza Miltonio, which hired only phase 1).
