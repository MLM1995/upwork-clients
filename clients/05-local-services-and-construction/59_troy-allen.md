## Project summary
Troy had just launched the BH Property Services website on Lovable, a contracting and property maintenance company in Halifax, and needed local authority starting from scratch. Marcos optimized the meta titles and descriptions of the key pages with competitor keywords and those given by the client: excavation, concrete repair, roofing, demolition, drywall, painting and commercial maintenance. He then created 25 citations and backlinks in Canadian directories using the client's Google Business listing and documented everything in a spreadsheet and a video. He also prepared an action plan for the Google Business listing and analyzed Brush Hour, his main business: third on Maps for "drywall halifax" and fifth or sixth on the web, with mostly toxic or nofollow backlinks.
