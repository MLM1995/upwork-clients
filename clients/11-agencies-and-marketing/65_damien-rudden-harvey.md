## Project summary
Damien, from an Edinburgh agency, used several small assignments as a trial before offering a retainer: raise Loft Conversions Edinburgh and recover the rankings of Joiner Edinburgh, which had suddenly dropped. On Joiner Edinburgh, Marcos detected and fixed an indexing problem: Cloudflare error 524 when reading the sitemaps, which he resolved with a cache rule. He also cleaned up the Yoast settings (feeds, headers, bots, parameters, internal searches), resubmitted all the sitemaps to Search Console and flagged noindex taxonomies inside the sitemap, 94 pages with 404s and 128 not indexed. When he tried to expand the scope because of the price, the client clarified that he only expected the indexing fix. On Loft Conversions, the earlier on-page work (another contract) produced 267% more clicks in 28 days and the average position went from 27.2 to 21.6.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Low-priced trial assignments in exchange for a possible monthly retainer with no selection process if there were measurable improvements
- The scope of this contract is only the indexing fix for Joiner Edinburgh; the rest of the technical SEO was left for the future retainer
- Recommended: an instant indexing plugin using the Google API (requires creating credentials in Google Cloud)
