## Project summary
Clarify was looking for a US Shopify store that makes more than 100,000 USD a month and would accept a free collaboration: the agency would analyze the store, implement one improvement for free (product page, sidecart, upsell) and measure it with an A/B test in order to publish it as a success story. Payment depended on finding a store that agreed. Marcos proposed a cold email campaign with Smartlead using his own mailboxes in addition to hello@clarify.software, and began building the lead lists. After analyzing the market he concluded that about 10,000 contacts would be needed to close a single store and cancelled on 22 July.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Results-based contract: 200 USD only if a store meeting the criteria accepts the collaboration; 10-day deadline
- Marcos is responsible for sourcing the leads
- Message focused on ecommerce experts who want their store to sell more, with the agency's reference cases (Zatorres, Canterville, Uniquely For) and the founder's previous experience (Pink Friday, MakeUpByMario)
- The client shared credentials in the chat
