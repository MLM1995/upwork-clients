## Project summary
Loft Conversions Edinburgh had been stuck on page 3 for "loft conversions edinburgh" for a long time, and the agency wanted to apply Page Optimizer Pro (POP) recommendations to the home page: word count, LSI terms, variations and heading structure. Marcos optimized the home page (on a backup copy), halved the repetition of the main keyword, which appeared in almost every block, fixed the identical headings on mobile and explained it in a video. The end client later asked for POP's "hyper aggressive" strategy (cutting the text to 824 words and matching 3 competitors). Marcos advised against it because of cannibalization and lack of semantic diversity, and the agency decided to give its changes time. This work later produced the 267% increase in clicks cited in contract 65.
