## Project summary
Joiner Edinburgh, a joinery business and client of Damien's agency, had dropped from position 2 or 3 to the bottom of page 2 for "joiner edinburgh" in 2 or 3 months with no apparent content changes. Marcos analyzed the site and delivered a video with the diagnosis. With access to Search Console he discovered that since April half of the pages were not being indexed (excluded by noindex and sitemaps that Google was not reading properly) and explained it in a second video for the end client. He offered to fix the indexing for 50 USD (which was done in contract 65) and proposed a retainer of 600 USD per month for 10 h or 900 USD for 15 h, which the agency made conditional on seeing results on the two sites.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Research for 20 USD; the indexing fix separately for 50 USD (contract 65)
- Proposed retainer at a reduced rate of 15 USD per hour to build trust; the agency preferred to focus it on on-site and technical SEO for all its clients
- The retainer decision depended on the results of Loft Conversions and Joiner Edinburgh within two weeks
- The client shared credentials in the chat
