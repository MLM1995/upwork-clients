## Project summary
Mauton, an agency owner, hired Marcos to execute the SEO audit already done for Mission Bowling Club, a bowling alley with a restaurant and events in San Francisco. Marcos implemented the on-page work (titles, metas, H1s and H2s, alt text, internal linking to Reserve a Lane, Parties and Corporate Events), added schema through code injection, submitted the sitemap, connected GTM with GA4 and analyzed Search Console. He explained that the 404s, the robots.txt blocks and the alternate canonicals were normal (iCal links, temporary waitlist pages and UTM parameters). Desktop speed rose to 94 and LCP to 1.1 s, with the help of an outside freelancer whom Marcos paid 50 EUR out of his own pocket; on mobile it stayed below 90 because of Squarespace's limits. He also created the Birthday Parties and Corporate Events landing pages, uploaded the gallery in Private Events and delivered a video with the 5-page design. Thanks to this work the site reached first place for "Bowling Club San Francisco".
