## Project summary
DMCenter launches about 4 cold email campaigns a month for B2B clients and was looking for someone to solve a spam trap problem, raise the open rate (around 15%) and manage campaigns long term. Since Apollo did not allow giving a teammate access without opening up the whole database, they agreed to move to Instantly. Marcos set up 5 mailboxes (on the fusionrsw.hu and dmcenter.hu domains), prepared the templates and launched a test campaign to 716 leads from the R&R FusionR account. After two weeks he exceeded the agreed KPI of more than 40% open rate, collected the milestone with a 20 USD bonus and proposed continuing with ClickUp and recurring work.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Payment conditional on achieving at least a 40% open rate after a two-week test
