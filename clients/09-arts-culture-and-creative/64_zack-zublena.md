## Project summary
Zack needed 25 email accounts spread across 5 domains of his gallery for cold email campaigns. The conversation before the contract took place outside this chat (by email). Marcos created the 25 accounts on his own hosting instead of Google Workspace or Zoho, configured the DNS in Cloudflare with help from his host's technical support, added the signatures by hand, connected them to Instantly for warm-up and sent a test email. He delivered on time with a video and a screenshot showing successful sending and receiving. The client wanted to talk by phone right afterwards, Marcos was not available, and the client closed the contract and requested a refund. Upwork resolved the dispute and Marcos received the 200 USD.

## Key decisions and agreements
- 25 mailboxes across 5 domains for 200 USD, delivered on 21 Jun
- Mailboxes hosted on Marcos's hosting to save the client between 1,800 and 3,600 USD per year compared with Google Workspace
- Account warm-up with Instantly
- The client shared credentials (Cloudflare) outside the Upwork chat
