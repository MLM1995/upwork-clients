## Project summary
Jenn wanted to modernize her portfolio with a custom Squarespace template inspired by lifeofmansoor.com, which she could edit herself (images, fonts and text). The structure: Design (by client and "View All"), Experiments, Archive (as a link with the text "Take me back in time"), Bio and Contact, with images linking to each client's pages. Marcos built the template in about 6 days. A mistake of his made the work-in-progress version public instead of a private duplicate while the client had open job applications; it was fixed the same day. Two features from the reference were not possible in Squarespace: infinite horizontal scroll and the identical archive section. The client approved the work after a review call.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Fixed price of 300 USD, the same as the initial offer even though the scope grew somewhat

- The landing video is in 16:9 format
- The current site stays visible; the work is done on a private duplicate (after the incident)
