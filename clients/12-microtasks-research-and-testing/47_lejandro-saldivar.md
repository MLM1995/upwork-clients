## Project summary
A Mindforall was preparing a new social network and was looking for Spanish-speaking university student influencers from Latin America on Instagram, with between 100,000 and 400,000 followers, currently enrolled students from modest backgrounds, with non-sexualized content and a real audience. Another freelancer had already built the main list, and Marcos was asked to extend it with 10 more profiles (a third) following the same requirements. Marcos proposed candidates and the client validated or rejected them as they came (for follower count, for not being a student or for signs of an affluent lifestyle). He completed the 10 valid profiles in the Google Sheet with each one's data.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Extension of 10 profiles for 10 USD
- Minimum of about 96,000 followers; profiles from UBA and UNAM count double
- Do not repeat profiles from the existing list; fill in only the grey columns of the sheet
- Brazilian profiles out of scope (the client accepted one as an exception)
