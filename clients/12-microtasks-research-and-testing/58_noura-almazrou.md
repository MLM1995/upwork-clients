## Project summary
Noura wanted a Google Sheet within 24 hours with all the LinkedIn employees of four real estate companies in Saudi Arabia and the UAE (Bayut KSA, Mada Properties, Rakez and Huspy, about 1,650 profiles), with more than 10 data points per person: job title, contract type, location, experience, university, education and contact. Marcos delivered a first extraction with basic data (name, URL, job title, location). Then his Sales Navigator account was blocked, and with Apollo not all profiles or all data came through (for example, 517 from Huspy instead of 750). Marcos proposed cancelling and the client closed the contract with a refund. The next day they discussed a possible TikTok scraping job (real estate videos in Arabic with engagement metrics, property type and area); Marcos ran a test with one account, but it was never contracted.

## Key decisions and agreements
- 30 USD for about 1,650 people (about 0.018 USD per lead), in two 50% milestones, with a maximum deadline of 24 hours
- One sheet per company and one row per person; empty cells if a data point is missing, nothing made up
- Cancelled because the complete data could not be obtained without Sales Navigator
