## Project summary
The client wanted 20 Reddit comments written as if from real users, naturally mentioning the VR ROCK brand (prescription lenses for Meta Quest 3 and PSVR 2) and its advantages: sharp lenses, fast and free shipping, good support and easy installation. The texts were to be filled into a Google Sheet, indicating whether each was a new comment or a reply to a specific user, without posting them yet. Marcos started writing them, but there was confusion about the sheet format (username versus community name). The client felt the texts sounded like AI and that one did not mention the brand, and asked to cancel after 30 minutes. Marcos closed the contract without being paid.
