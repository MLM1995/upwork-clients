## Project summary
TracklistPro, a tool for transferring playlists between Spotify, Apple Music and other platforms, paid 0.20 USD for each reply in forums, Twitter, Quora or Reddit recommending the tool in threads where people asked how to move their playlists, with limits per platform and delivery of an Excel file of URLs. Marcos accepted 25 replies for 5 USD, sent the first ones for review and completed all 25 live on 2 May. The client was slow to respond; Marcos asked for payment on 27 May and closed the contract the next day, so the client could no longer pay. When the client offered to pay if he applied again, Marcos declined.

## Key decisions and agreements
- 5 USD for 25 replies, with a possible increase to 0.30 USD per reply and unlimited long-term work
- Do not reply in threads where TracklistPro was already mentioned; 1 or 2 replies per forum; maximum of 2 on Quora and 2 on Reddit
- The contract was closed before payment and Marcos gave up the 5 USD
