## Project summary
Spanish voice data micro-tasks. First, Marcos reviewed whether recordings at 8 kHz and 16 kHz matched their transcriptions and flagged errors (the URL with "triple double-u", missing commas, the address in English). He then recorded 10 sentences in WAV format in Audacity (numbers, alphanumeric codes, phone numbers, currencies, dates, times, units, URLs, social media and addresses), correcting two sentences that sounded unnatural in Spanish. He was paid the 15 USD for this pilot test after a two-week wait for the quality review. The second milestone (a natural 8-minute conversation about family with background noise, for 10 USD) was rejected because Marcos recorded alone and without noise, and the client closed the contract with a refund. They also offered him a long transcription review for 10 USD, which Marcos considered underpaid.

## Key decisions and agreements
- 15 USD pilot paid only after internal quality control approval
- WAV recordings at 8 or 16 kHz with a word error rate below 5%
- The family conversation had to be between two people, natural, with background noise and 10 seconds of silence at the start and end
- Marcos turned down several tasks because of the low rate and slow payments
