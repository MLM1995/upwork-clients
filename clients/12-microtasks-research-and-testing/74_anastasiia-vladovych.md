## Project summary
Mystery shopper visit to a C&A store in Salzburg to evaluate customer service: look for a pair of jeans, try them on, ask the staff three questions, check size availability, spend at least 30 minutes in the store and buy something for about 5 EUR, with 4 photos and a report in German, in the first person and past tense. Marcos did the visit and the report, but at the wrong store (Rainerstraße instead of Europark). He repeated it at the correct store and filled in the survey on the Shopmetrics platform after negotiating an extra 5.58 USD to cover the new purchase. He was paid after the end client's validation.
