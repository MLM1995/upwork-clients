## Project summary
Matthew needed the website for a new apartment complex, copying the style and layout of thewyldes.com but with Madison View's branding, copy, images, fonts (Merriweather and Raleway) and colors according to an instructions PDF. Marcos first built the replica, which the client considered insufficient because the branding had not been applied, and then customized the whole site in WordPress on GoDaddy. It includes the Floor Plans, Amenities, Location, Availability (table with floor plans in a pop-up), Gallery, Virtual Tour and Contact pages, plus a form, mobile version and basic SEO (metas based on the competition). The client himself made several final adjustments (menus, slugs, buttons) when Marcos considered them out of scope. Project delivered in 9 days; Marcos later uses it as a portfolio example.
