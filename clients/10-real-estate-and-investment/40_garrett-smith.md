## Project summary
ReefHaven Ventures needed an institutional 5-page website on Squarespace (Wells template) that would build trust with its investors (LPs), on a very tight deadline between the end of the year and 8 January. Marcos first designed a prototype in Lovable (design-buddy-bot-57.lovable.app) based on references such as AIX Ventures, Balderton, Pear and Phase Two and the UC San Diego blue palette. He generated AI coral reef images for the hero and iterated through several rounds of feedback (V2 to V7) while moving the design to Squarespace. Meetings were missed or delayed because of Marcos's travel (Doha and Dubai), the site was accidentally published and then password-protected, and the client complained about spacing, font sizes and inconsistent links. On 9 January, with investor meetings underway, Garrett said he was disappointed and would finish the adjustments himself in Squarespace; Marcos agreed and the second milestone was not paid.

## Key decisions and agreements
- The client asked to lower the price to 1,500 USD and it stayed at 1,800 USD fixed price (60% at the start and 40% on delivery), instead of the hourly offer at 34 USD
- Brand imagery: coral reef (not rocky), in black and white or muted blue, conveying ecosystem and stability, nothing tropical or glossy tech aesthetic
- Colors inspired by UCSD (blues and white, a minimal touch of gold)
- Round team photos; AIX-style footer
- Feedback via Google Docs and videos; the clients do not edit Squarespace while Marcos is working (until 9 January)
- The site was not to be published until finished

## Open items or notes for the future
- Project closed with an unhappy client; the client did not leave a review
- Lessons: do not accept such short deadlines when they coincide with travel, do not miss meetings, and build the design directly on the final platform instead of in a separate prototype
