## Project summary
Get Blue, a longevity platform with a personalized biomarker map, hired Marcos to set up the sign-up and membership flow in Webflow with Memberstack 2.0 and Stripe. Marcos built the complete funnel (sign-up at /start-today, plan selection at /membership-pricing, payment with Stripe Hosted Checkout in three tiers and access to /dashboard) and verified that Stripe was connected in live mode with the three price IDs active; only publishing the pricing page on the domain was missing. He also delivered a PDF case study (v3.1, 30 pages, with a go-live checklist). The client rejected the final milestone, claiming that the changes from the 19 May call were not completed, that there was no end-to-end testing and that Marcos missed the closing call, and opened a dispute. Marcos accepted Upwork's non-binding recommendation (75/25) to avoid arbitration, and the contract was closed on 1 June.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Fixed price of 800 USD in two milestones of 400 USD
- Checkout simplified to three tiers (references to 4 tiers, semiannual and "Most Popular" were removed)
- Marcos pointed out a mobile performance score of 31 as a bottleneck in the funnel and offered to fix it separately by the hour; the client considered it outside the engagement
- Dispute in the Resolution Center: Marcos accepted the 75/25 split proposed by the mediator (Karen) so as not to pay 337,50 USD of arbitration each party
