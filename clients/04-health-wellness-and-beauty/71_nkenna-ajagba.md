## Project summary
Collaboration of more than a year to give local visibility to an integrative psychiatry practice for women in Austin (reproductive, integrative, Christian, maternal and women's psychiatry). It started with 4 weeks of service pages and blogs and continued with biweekly plans of 100 to 200 USD. The work included keyword research against competitors such as rpcaustin.com and integrativepsychiatryaustin.com, service and condition pages (PMDD, perimenopause, OCD, anxiety, depression, eating disorders, PCOS and endometriosis, pregnancy and postpartum), about 15 blogs, schema on the main pages, internal linking, redesign of the footer and of the Who I Help, Resources and Contact pages, optimization and posts on Google Business Profile, listings on Bing, Yelp, Foursquare and MapQuest, and citations and backlinks (for example, PSI Directory with DR 69). Results cited: average position from 38 to 18 in Search Console (September 2025) and an average position of 5,4 on newly published condition pages (February 2026). The client ended it in April 2026 because the business was slowing down and she considered that enough had been done.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Communication only through Upwork; the client approves everything before publishing and writes many texts herself to keep her voice
- Keep religious content separate: faith only on the Christian psychiatry page, which is not linked in the menu; elsewhere, spirituality without references to faith
- Images of women of different ethnicities and ages; without the doctor's photo on every page
- No public email or WhatsApp in the listings; nkennaconnects@gmail.com was created only for the citations
- Biweekly rhythm and, from October 2025, 200 USD per two weeks (Marcos lowered his rate to about 17 USD per hour); in March 2026, 30 USD per hour with a maximum of 15 h
- Merging the women's psychiatry pages into a single one, "Women's Mental Health / Women's Holistic Psychiatry", with 301 redirects
- Marcos proposed getting paid Google reviews and even paid for 5 himself; the client asked that they be original and then that no more be done
- The client shared credentials in the chat
