## Project summary
Nina wanted more local visibility to attract clients and above all stylists (chair rental, commission and apprentices). Marcos ran an audit with double keyword research (services and recruitment), optimized the Google Business listing (corrected categories, new photos, logo), on-page work in Wix (titles, metas, headings, alt text, embedded map), a misconfigured URL, citations and about 25 backlinks, and connected GA4 and Search Console. He discovered that the business was formerly called Salon Rache and asked for the old citations that confused Google to be removed. Results: from position 15 to 3 on the web for "hair salon campbell" and to 6 on Maps. In the January maintenance he built more than 25 citations, responded to reviews, added geotagged photos, posts and Bing Webmaster Tools; in February he reported 20 % more sessions (815) and 29 % more organic traffic.
