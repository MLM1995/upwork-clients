## Project summary
Jeff was about to launch the website for the new residential program, built by GoDaddy with Beaver Builder and in "coming soon" mode, and came from a bad experience on Fiverr: the previous freelancer stuffed the home page with "drug rehab" to turn Yoast green. Marcos ran an audit with competitor analysis (Ahrefs) and detected the lack of service pages by search intent, an unclear architecture and a message more focused on housing than on treatment. He rewrote the home page, optimized and linked the service pages (drug rehab, residential treatment, inpatient rehab and addiction treatment in Phoenix, plus Arizona-level searches), configured Yoast (titles, schema, sitemap, indexing) and connected Search Console and Analytics. At close, all pages had a double green light in Yoast and he delivered a PDF report and three explanatory videos. The client approved the payment and left a review.
