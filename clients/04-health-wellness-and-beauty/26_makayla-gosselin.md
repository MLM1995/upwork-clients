## Project summary
Makayla, a previous SEO client (work in February), came back because her email bookme@makaylabeautyy.com had stopped working when the old domain expired on Wix. After she bought the domain back, Marcos reactivated the old mailbox, checked that nothing had been lost (4.848 emails, including the 2026 and 2027 brides) and set up the new bookme@makaylabeauty.com in Google Workspace with automatic forwarding from the old address. The main blocker was verifying the domain in Google: Square's DNS panel would not save the TXT record and Square support could not do it; in the end Makayla resolved it by phone with Square and Google and the domain was verified on 29 or 30 April. Marcos completed the forwarding, tested it with an email that reached both mailboxes and the client approved the milestone. Afterwards Marcos sent her two SEO proposals to recover rankings against local competitors, with no reply.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Marcos first proposed a starter SEO plan for 450 USD and shared his retainer package; the client had no budget

- Forwarding from bookme@makaylabeautyy.com to bookme@makaylabeauty.com, which becomes the main professional email
