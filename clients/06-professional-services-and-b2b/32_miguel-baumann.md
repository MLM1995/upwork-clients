## Project summary
Miguel had two reports (an SEO audit and a list of technical fixes) and was looking for someone to implement them on his WordPress site, which runs the Avada theme on SiteGround. Marcos did it in about two days: JSON-LD schema (Person, ProfessionalService/LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, AggregateRating with his 22 Google reviews), new robots.txt and llms.txt files, titles and meta descriptions for 7 pages, copy changes on the home page and a duplicate H1 fixed, a 301 redirect for /confidence-coaching-old/, a LinkedIn link, alt text fixed in bulk, the repeated XML declaration reduced to a single one, and the sitemap resubmitted in Search Console. He explained why there was no need to touch the duplicate navigation (it is normal Avada behavior for desktop and mobile) or the LinkedIn pixel (it was already loading asynchronously). All schema was validated in the Rich Results Test. Performance: 96 on desktop and 58 on mobile.
