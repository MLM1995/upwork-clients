## Project summary
MagsFood sells Cameroonian palm oil (brands Eposi, Lum and Engwari) and other African products to the diaspora in the US. Marcos carried out a phased plan: technical and on-page SEO (H1, metas, schema, alt text, sitemap), speed and image compression, Klaviyo with post-purchase, welcome, repeat and win-back flows, Judge.me for reviews with widgets on all product pages, Google Merchant Center and GA4, and a Google Ads account set up but without campaigns. He also redesigned the Shop page (one row per brand with its 4 sizes), swapped the product photos for the new labels, improved the contrast of the menu and the hero, and redirected all notifications to Mairo's email. Results cited: "excellent" speed performance, 19 products approved in Merchant Center and 175 % more sessions since 1 March. After the close, the domain went down in May because of a DNS change by the server developer; Marcos diagnosed NXDOMAIN, ran a post-restoration audit (12-page report) and, with a later block of 9 hours for 350 USD, published the palm oil pillar guide, three recipes (Ghanaian jollof, Senegalese jollof and pallava soup), rebuilt the video block, replaced the testimonials carousel with the Judge.me widget and redid the contact page. At that point GSC showed a CTR of 12,9 % and an average position of 9,6.
