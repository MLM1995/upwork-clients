## Project summary
Nathan wanted a WordPress landing page for his upcoming restaurant with three goals: signing up for pop-ups and events, receiving notices of the opening date and promoting his Instagram and TikTok, plus a contact page. Marcos analyzed the client's ChatGPT mockups and recommended leaving the social networks for the end so as not to take clicks away from the sign-up. He delivered two design drafts as images, but had trouble applying the requested changes (logo, contact page, Montserrat font and adjustments from a screenshot). When he saw that the client expected the site built in WordPress, Marcos said the 50 USD only covered the design. They closed the job by mutual agreement with full payment but no review.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Fixed price of 50 USD in two milestones of 25 USD
- Social networks at the bottom of the page and a single main sign-up button
- Freedom to change the colors
- Work stopped because of a lack of agreement on whether the price included the WordPress implementation
- The client shared credentials in the chat
