## Project summary
Nico wanted more direct orders and more catering requests in Brisbane. Marcos proposed a three-phase plan (on-page and conversion, Google Business Profile with citations, and technical speed), and Nico hired only the first. In the initial analysis Marcos found that the previous agency had created hundreds of landing pages by keyword and neighborhood on the old WordPress site (doorway style) and that, after Nico's own migration to Square in January, more than 300 URLs returned 404 in Search Console. He reviewed the WordPress backup at VentraIP, which had no database, and proposed a separate recovery, which Nico postponed. Within phase 1 Marcos restructured the catering page and aligned the home page with searches such as "pizza catering Brisbane": keyword research, H1 to H3 hierarchy, titles and metas, internal linking, quote and call CTAs, an expanded quote form, "Most Popular" and "Best Value" labels on the packages, and placement of the new photos. Baseline measurement for phase 2: PageSpeed of 14 on mobile (LCP of 20,2 s) and 15 on desktop.

## Key decisions and agreements
- Initial price of 550 USD for on-page and technical work; the client asked to reduce the scope and phase 1 was set at 385 USD, which ended up being 375
- Direct implementation in Square and in Google Business Profile, not just recommendations
- Do not recover the hundreds of duplicate pages; better to have a few solid pages
- Phase 2 (speed on home, catering and menu, with a target of 30 to 50+ on mobile) for 550 USD; Nico asked to lower it to 350 or to 475 with photos included, Marcos kept the price and the client did not continue
- The client shared credentials in the chat (VentraIP access and verification codes)
