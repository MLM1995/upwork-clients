2. Scope of work

A. Setup & information architecture

  • Saro remixed into your Framer workspace. Brand colors, text styles and buttons set once in the design system.
  • Sitemap and navigation agreed with you before any build. The mega menu groups Product, Solutions (industries and regions) and Company.
  • Global header, mega menu, footer, CTA blocks and 404 page.

B. Page build & content migration (46 pages)

  • 11 core pages built individually on the matching Saro layouts, with all existing copy migrated and sections adapted to your content.
  • 17 product pages built on one reusable Framer product layout made from Saro's product pages, then filled page by page. You can add future products in minutes.
  • 12 industry, region and partner pages built as a Solutions CMS collection, so your team edits them like database entries.
  • 6 legal pages migrated as formatted text pages.
  • Image placeholders marked with the sizes your design team needs. Current images kept wherever they still fit.

C. Blog migration (151 posts)

  • Posts exported from WordPress, cleaned of Elementor markup, and imported into the Framer Blog CMS with title, slug, date, author, category, featured image and body.
  • Formatting spot-checked on a sample of posts, and internal links updated to the new URLs.

D. Forms & integrations

  • Contact, Get Started and newsletter forms rebuilt in Framer and connected to HubSpot, which your site already uses, or another destination you choose.
  • Thank-you page, the HubSpot tracking script and GA4/GTM moved over.

E. Responsive, SEO & launch

  • Every page checked and adjusted on desktop, tablet and mobile.
  • Current SEO titles and meta descriptions migrated (not rewritten), plus alt text, Open Graph images and clean slugs that match the current URLs wherever possible.
  • Full 301 redirect map of about 200 URLs, so you keep your Google rankings and backlinks.
  • Pre-launch QA (links, forms, speed, favicon, sitemap), domain connection, Google Search Console resubmission and a 7-day post-launch check.
  • Recorded handover video showing how your team edits pages, the CMS and the blog.
